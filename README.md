## 🌌 What is Vynta?

**Vynta** is not merely a task manager; it is a **Proactive AI Temporal Flow Engine**. It bridges the gap between *what* you need to do and *when* you are biologically optimized to do it. Vynta transforms plain-language intentions into precise, energy-aware schedules.

Built by [Murshid R](https://linkedin.com/in/murshid-r-37088b272), Vynta represents a shift from static list-making to dynamic, AI-assisted scheduling using cutting-edge Large Language Models (LLMs).

---

## ✨ Core Pillars

### 🧠 Natural Language Intelligence
Users don't fill out forms; they "speak" to the system. Vynta's Neural Core extracts titles, durations, intents, and temporal constraints from raw text.

### ⚡ Energy-Aware Scheduling
Vynta aligns your workload with your circadian rhythm:
- **High (Deep Work):** Coding, Writing, Analysis.
- **Medium (Operational):** Meetings, Emails.
- **Low (Administrative):** Chores, Organization.

### 🔄 Adaptive Reflow
Life is chaotic. When your schedule breaks, Vynta's **Adaptive Reflow** re-balances the remaining day, shattering delayed tasks into smaller subtasks and recalculating your "Temporal Flow" in real-time.

## 🚀 Installation & Usage

1. **Download:** Go to the [Releases](https://github.com/quantumstack-labs/Vynta/releases) section and download the latest `.apk` file.
2. **Install:** Open the file on your Android device (ensure "Install from Unknown Sources" is enabled).
3. **Setup:** Log in with Google to sync your calendar and configure your "Energy Windows" in settings.
